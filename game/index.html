Sunset Bay

A GTA 6 style open world. Fourteen districts, one golden hour, and a crew waiting for its next big score.

Best on desktop Chrome / Edge with WebGL. Your progress saves automatically to this browser.

How to Play

WASD walk / drive  ·  Shift sprint  ·  Mouse: look around (click the game to lock the mouse)

E steal the nearest car / get out  ·  Space brake  ·  R respray your car ($150)  ·  F interact

1 2 3 switch weapon  ·  Left click: shoot  ·  Q next radio station  ·  M mute  ·  H horn

Esc / P pause. Follow the yellow marker on the minimap to reach your mission. Stealing cars and shooting raise your wanted level — hide from the police to cool down. Earn cash from missions, taxi and courier jobs, street races, and the big three-phase heist. Build respect in all 14 districts to unlock better prices and missions.
